# Kaftan Angelo — Product Marketing Context

**Version:** v1 (2026-11-17)  
**Status:** Website-grounded draft. Confirm sales hypotheses against actual store/customer data before broad campaigns.

## What the business offers

Kaftan Angelo is a physical premium leather, shearling and fur outerwear store at Kossuth Lajos u. 18, 1053 Budapest. The HU/EN website showcases selected models and guides, then directs visitors to in-store fitting or a WhatsApp conversation. It does not sell through an online cart and does not publish prices or real-time inventory. The store also discusses individual sizing/custom orders and operates a tourism-partner programme.

## Buyer segments and jobs

| Segment | Job to be done | Useful next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budapest resident | Compare materials and fitting in person | View model, ask about size, visit store |
| Short-stay visitor | Find a central, approachable store within a trip | View English catalogue, get directions |
| Customer needing an adjustment | Understand options for a particular garment | Ask the store for an individual consultation |
| Hotel, concierge or tour operator | Recommend relevant shopping to guests | Discuss partner arrangements through the partner page |

## Why / positioning hypothesis

Help shoppers feel more confident in a consequential outerwear purchase by making the material, fit, location and opportunities to ask questions clear before a store visit.

Possible differentiation to validate: central Budapest showroom + two-language discovery + model-specific enquiries + personal fittings. Do not assert that alternatives lack these benefits without competitor research.

## Primary conversion paths

1. Search / homepage → category → model → WhatsApp product question → store confirmation → possible visit / sale.
2. Search / store information → directions or phone → possible visit / sale.
3. Partner landing page → genuine enquiry → agreed partnership terms → referred visit / sale.
4. Guide → relevant product/category → enquiry or visit.

**Measurement boundary:** A WhatsApp click, phone click, directions click, form submission attempt and completed sale are different events. A fallback email form opens the visitor's email application; it does not confirm delivery. Store sales require separate first-party evidence.

## Messaging guidelines

- Give verified product and material details, practical opening hours and simple directions.
- Be helpful and precise about current availability, fit and custom-order timing: the store must confirm these individually.
- Avoid urgency, invented reviews, invented stock, unverifiable numerical claims and promised universal shipping/alteration terms.
- Keep HU and EN language natural. Preserve the business rules in the repository's root `AGENTS.md`.

## Claims that need periodic owner confirmation

- The historical opening year and other heritage statements.
- Ratings and partner-network counts displayed on the site.
- Garment-specific materials, adjustment options, and any delivery/service assurances.

## Priorities

1. Confirm that core contact and store-direction flows work on mobile.
2. Measure consented **intent** events without collecting the contents of customer messages or partner forms.
3. Verify HU/EN indexing and the real-world quality of traffic through Search Console / Analytics when available.
4. Research the top recurring questions before creating new guides or changing positioning.
5. Validate the partner proposition through real business discussions, without automated outbound sends.

## Open data questions

- How many qualified WhatsApp conversations result in store visits?
- Which categories lead to real sales, and from which language/channel?
- How are partner referrals recorded and settled?
- What are the true customer objections and frequently requested fits/sizes?
