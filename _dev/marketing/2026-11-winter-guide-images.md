# Winter guide photography

The eight seasonal guides reuse existing model photography, matched to actual model slugs and recorded dimensions from src/data/models.json. The four owner-approved editorial covers illustrate styling ideas; they are not photographs of specific catalogue products, verified store interiors or documentary evidence of a location. The existing optimizer creates AVIF and WebP sizes from the same original; both languages share assets. Product images have localized descriptive alt text, linked captions, intrinsic dimensions, lazy loading and responsive candidates. The new covers use the existing responsive hero preloading; seasonal image arrays are emitted in Article structured data and the sitemap.
