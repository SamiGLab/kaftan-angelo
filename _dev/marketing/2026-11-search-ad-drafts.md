## Negative-query candidates to review

- HU: `ingyen`, `használt`, `bőrkabát tisztítás`, `bőrkabát javítás`, `műbőr` when searches are for faux leather only.
- EN: `free`, `second hand`, `used`, `jacket repair`, `leather cleaning`, `faux leather` when unrelated to genuine-leather retail.
- **Do not use** overly broad negatives such as `custom`, `size`, `shearling`, `women` or `men`; they are relevant for this shop.

Review actual search-term reports instead of blindly importing the full list.

## Launch checklist — hard blockers

- [ ] Owner approves campaign language, territory, monthly spending cap and billing account.
- [ ] Google Ads access/permission provided; no impersonation or unapproved spend.
- [ ] Profile address, phone, open hours and public claims independently verified.
- [ ] GA4/GTM conversion actions checked; label website **contact start** separately from confirmed enquiries.
- [ ] Manual aggregate store-visit attribution and sales tally agreed, without uploading private message text.
- [ ] Landing pages tested mobile in both languages; privacy consent remains opt-in.
- [ ] Policy and ad character limits checked in the actual ad platform at upload.
- [ ] First review after meaningful real search terms; pause irrelevant queries and adjust without guessing results.

No “sales”, “purchases” or “applications delivered” conversion value should be attributed to a click. No specific stock, price, promotion or guaranteed alteration is advertised.
