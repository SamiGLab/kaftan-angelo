# Partner enquiry privacy notice — HU/EN review package

Version: `partner-enquiry-2026-10-17-draft`. **Not approved for publication or form activation.** This is a source-backed completeness review and draft, not external legal sign-off. Controller identity/address and mailbox below come from the current site and owner instruction; verify their accuracy before publication.

## Publication blockers

- Confirm the lawful basis for each applicant type. A corporate representative is not automatically a party to a prospective contract: Article 6(1)(b) cannot be assumed for every B2B contact. If relying on Article 6(1)(f), document the purpose, necessity, balancing and objection route before adopting the proposed wording below. Notice acknowledgement is not marketing consent.
- Confirm actual Supabase project region, contracted processors/subprocessors, Brevo use, operator mailbox provider, transfers and safeguards. Publish actual recipient/transfer information, not a claim that everything remains in the EU. Turnstile and Brevo adapters are staged, not active.
- Establish backup expiry, mailbox/provider copy handling, status handoff and authorized holds. No promise of erasure from every system at exactly day 90 is supportable yet. The approved rule makes unconverted enquiries eligible at 90 days; operating frequency and residual copies must be explained.
- Confirm who handles rights requests, whether a DPO applies, the final notice URL/version and dated controller/legal review. No new DPO, legal retention duration for signed records, or processor contract is invented here.

## English proposed notice

**Partner enquiries — proposed future service.** Controller: Alfina Fashion Kft., Kossuth Lajos u. 18, 1053 Budapest. Contact: alfinafashionkft@gmail.com. We use your name, organisation, role/category, email, optional telephone number, message and language to assess and respond to a proposed partnership. Required fields are needed to handle your request; submitting a request is voluntary and does not subscribe you to marketing. Please do not include identity documents, bank details or sensitive personal information.

Proposed basis, subject to the review above: our legitimate interest in evaluating and responding to business enquiries; where you personally request steps toward your own contract, the applicable pre-contractual basis may instead apply. We do not make solely automated partnership decisions. Authorized staff handle enquiries. The final notice must identify the actual hosting, anti-abuse, notification and mailbox recipients, relevant international transfers and safeguards before this service opens.

An enquiry that has not become a signed partnership becomes eligible for deletion 90 days after its server-recorded creation; further contact does not restart that period. A documented legal obligation or active dispute may justify a restricted, time-limited hold. Signed partnership records, agreements, sales and commissions follow a separate process and are not deleted by this enquiry cleanup. The published operating schedule and expiry of restricted backup, email and provider copies must be supplied before activation. A database deletion does not recall previously delivered email.

You may contact us to request access, correction, erasure or restriction and, where applicable, portability or to object to processing. We normally respond within one month, subject to the lawful extension rules. You may complain to the Hungarian supervisory authority, [NAIH](https://www.naih.hu/). Acknowledging this notice is distinct from consenting to newsletters. Until the service is activated, the existing email/WhatsApp handoff remains in use and is subject to those providers' processing.
