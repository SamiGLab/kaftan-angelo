> **Quick answer:** Pack a warm coat, a removable mid-layer, scarf, gloves and comfortable shoes with good grip. Standing at a Christmas market feels different from walking briskly through Budapest.

If you plan to visit [Vörösmarty Classic Xmas](https://vorosmartyclassicxmas.hu/) and [Advent Basilica](https://adventbazilika.hu/en), consider how long you will be outdoors and whether you will stop at cafés or restaurants. Check the organisers' websites for current opening times and programmes.

## How cold does Budapest feel in December?

Mornings and evenings near freezing are possible, although conditions vary by day. Check the forecast before travelling and again before heading out in the evening, particularly for wind and precipitation. You may feel warm on the metro and cold again while standing at a market stall. Removable layers help you adapt.

## Three outfits for a winter city break

- **Daytime market visit:** a base layer, knitwear, warm coat, comfortable trousers and shoes suited to damp paving.
- **Evening at Advent Basilica:** a warmer mid-layer, coat that closes around the neck, scarf, hat and gloves. Consider a longer coat for extra coverage.
- **Sightseeing followed by dinner:** dark trousers, a plain knit and a lined leather or shearling coat appropriate for the weather.

## Is a leather jacket warm enough?

A thin leather jacket is better suited to mild conditions or short trips between indoor venues. A lined leather coat or shearling style may be more practical for a longer outdoor visit. Construction, coverage, layers and the weather all matter. Leather and shearling are not automatically waterproof.

If you already own a suitable winter coat, bring it. There is no need to buy another one just for a market visit.

## Packing checklist for a long weekend

One warm coat, two mid-layers, a change of base-layer clothing, comfortable trousers, warm socks, scarf, hat, gloves and supportive footwear. Bring an umbrella or suitable weather protection if precipitation is forecast.
