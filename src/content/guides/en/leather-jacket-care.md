Genuine leather is a natural material, so it does not behave like a synthetic jacket. The main rule is simple: **do not soak it, do not dry it on a radiator, and do not use unknown aggressive cleaners.**

## 1. Dust and surface cleaning
Use a soft dry cloth regularly. A slightly damp cloth can be used carefully on a small area.

## 2. If the jacket gets wet
Let it dry naturally on a hanger at room temperature. Direct heat can dry the leather out and make it stiff.

## 3. Storage
Use a wide hanger and avoid compressing it for long periods. Leather benefits from ventilation.

## 4. Conditioning
Use a conditioner suitable for the specific leather type and test it on a discreet area first.
