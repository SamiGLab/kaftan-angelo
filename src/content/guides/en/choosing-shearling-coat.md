> **Quick answer:** Try the individual shearling coat with your intended sweater. Length, weight, fastening and movement matter together; the category name does not establish identical warmth across models.

## Read the material description
Ask what the outer body, inner side and any trims are made from. Shearling combines skin and its wool side; contrasting collars or linings may need different care. Do not infer composition from a photograph.

## Length and everyday use
In a shorter cut, check waist coverage while seated. Walk, take ordinary steps and sit in a longer coat. Consider how much you walk, travel or drive: the length and weight should suit your own day.

## Sweater, collar and cuffs
Bring your intended layer. Fasten the coat, reach forward and check neck, shoulder and armhole comfort. The collar should not press uncomfortably; cuffs and sleeves should provide appropriate coverage during movement. Do not choose a painfully tight fit.

## Questions before buying
- The material and finish of the individual model.
- Availability of your preferred size and colour.
- The care label and cleaning requirements.
- Whether a proposed adjustment is feasible.

Do not infer a universal temperature guarantee: cut, coverage, layers and wearing conditions affect comfort too.

## Care and storage
Part of choosing a coat is knowing whether you can follow its own instructions. Do not automatically apply your leather-jacket cream to shearling or its wool side. Read the [shearling care guide](/en/guides/shearling-coat-care/).
