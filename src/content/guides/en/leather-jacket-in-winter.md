> **Quick answer:** A leather jacket can work in winter when its lining, length and your clothing layers suit the weather. For longer outdoor outings, compare a lined leather coat with a shearling coat.

Driving to work, waiting for a tram and spending an evening walking beside the Danube are very different situations. The right jacket is the one that suits the way you actually spend your day.

## What makes a leather jacket suitable for winter?

The leather exterior alone tells you little about warmth. Look at the lining, front closure, collar and body length. A shorter jacket may be comfortable while driving, whereas a longer cut may offer more coverage during a walk.

If you plan to wear a thick sweater, check that there is room across the shoulders and upper arms. Fasten the jacket during the fitting: you will not always wear it open in winter.

## Three winter situations, three options

- **Driving and short trips:** a lined leather jacket that feels comfortable while seated and reaching forward.
- **Commuting:** a hip-covering coat with a closable neck, tried with your usual knitwear and scarf.
- **Long outdoor visits:** compare a longer winter coat with a shearling style, taking weight, cuff coverage and neck closure into account.

The wool side of genuine shearling can provide insulation. The warmth of an individual coat depends on its construction, fit, coverage and conditions. Leather and shearling are not automatically waterproof; check the garment's own care instructions before exposure to rain or snow.

## A two-minute fitting with your winter sweater

Bring the knit you expect to wear in January. Fasten the coat, raise your arms and sit down. Check whether your waist and wrists remain comfortably covered. If you are deciding between two cuts, try them with the same sweater so the comparison is meaningful.
