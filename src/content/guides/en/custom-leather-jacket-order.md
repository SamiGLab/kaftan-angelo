> **Quick answer:** Bring a reference photo and explain how you want to wear the jacket. Discuss the available material, cut, measurements, price and expected timing with the store before deciding. Custom leather jacket enquiries are available at Kaftan Angelo, Kossuth Lajos u. 18, Budapest 1053.

## When is a custom order worth discussing?
If the collection does not offer the combination of cut, colour or fit you want, ask about a custom leather jacket. A reference photo helps explain your preference, but does not establish that an identical design or material can be supplied.

## What should you prepare before visiting?
- One or two reference photos, including details such as the collar and fastening.
- Your preferred colour, approximate length and everyday use.
- The sweater or other layer you expect to wear underneath.
- Your budget and any date by which you hope to have the jacket.

First [browse the collection](/en/collections/): an existing model may already suit you. If you have a particular piece in mind, send its photo to the store and ask about availability before travelling.

## Which measurements are needed?
Chest, sleeve and body length are useful discussion points, but the final requirements depend on the cut. A usual size label or self-measurement does not confirm the fit. Use the [fit checklist](/en/guides/leather-jacket-fit-guide/) to prepare for the conversation, and ask the store how measurements and any fitting should be arranged.

## How much does a custom jacket cost, and how long does it take?
Ask for the price and estimated completion time for your particular request. Confirm the material, design details, payment arrangements and any fitting steps before placing an order. There is no single published price or guaranteed production time on this website.

## Can a visitor to Budapest enquire?
Yes: explain your travel dates when contacting the store. Discuss whether the proposed timing and any fitting requirements suit your visit; do not assume completion before your departure or overseas delivery.
