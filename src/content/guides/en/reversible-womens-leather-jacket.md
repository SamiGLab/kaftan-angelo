Choosing between a plain everyday jacket and something more expressive? A reversible jacket gives you both looks. Before deciding, consider how each side would work with the clothes you already wear.

## See both sides

The [Kaftan Angelo reversible women’s leather jacket gallery](/en/models/reversible-womens-leather-jacket-budapest/) shows red, cream, orange, green and black versions. Four photographs per colour show the plain and patterned sides from the front and back.

| If you want | What to compare in the photos |
| --- | --- |
| An understated everyday look | The plain side with your usual trousers and bag |
| A more expressive outfit | The patterned side with plain clothing |
| Different looks while travelling | Both sides with a few pieces you plan to pack |

Photographed colours do not establish current availability.

## Try it on both ways

Reverse the jacket and try it with each side facing out. Check whether the zip is easy to use, the neckline and trim feel comfortable, and the pockets sit where you want them. Look at it open and closed over the layer you normally wear.

Compare it with a conventional [women’s leather jacket](/en/womens-leather-jackets/) too. If you would rarely wear one side of the reversible design, a different cut may suit your wardrobe better.

## Ask about care

Ask about the material on both sides of the specific garment and follow its care label. Do not assume both surfaces can be treated in the same way. The [leather care guide](/en/guides/leather-jacket-care/) can help you prepare questions.
