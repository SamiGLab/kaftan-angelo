> **Quick answer:** An elegant leather jacket becomes more versatile when it works with the clothes you already own. Tailored trousers, a knit or a midi dress can take the same jacket from one occasion to another.

You do not necessarily need a new wardrobe. Start with the clothes you actually wear and the occasions you have in mind.

## 1. Leather blazer with tailored trousers

A clean blazer-style cut can work for the office with a cream knit, straight-leg tailored trousers and simple ankle boots. In cold weather a lighter blazer may need a separate winter coat over it. Sit down during the fitting to check the shoulders.

## 2. Belted leather jacket with a midi dress

A belted style can stand out against a single-colour dress. Try a shorter jacket with a fuller skirt, and compare a longer cut with a straight dress. With a light-coloured jacket, think about whether it also works with your regular shoes and bags.

## 3. Black leather jacket with jeans

Dark jeans, an off-white knit and black boots make an uncomplicated city-weekend outfit. If the jacket has prominent zips or embellishments, keep the other pieces simple. Try the jacket with a winter sweater, not just a thin top.

## 4. Coloured suede with neutral basics

A red, green or blue jacket can become the focus of a wardrobe built around black, brown and beige. With suede, ask how to care for the individual finish, particularly in wet weather.

## 5. Reversible jacket for a varied day

Going from work to dinner, or packing light for a trip? A reversible jacket can offer two different looks. Check the fastening, pockets and fit on both sides before deciding.
