> **Quick answer:** A textured winter coat often needs little more than a plain knit, simple trousers and boots. Choose the length, weight and styling details according to the occasions you have in mind.

A short fur jacket, a longer coat, a vest and a fur-trimmed style can create very different looks. They need not be reserved for evening wear.

## A short fur jacket for city days

A hip-length style can work with straight or slightly wide-leg trousers. Try a cream knit and dark trousers with a brown jacket. If the top half has a full silhouette, understated boots and a simple bag can balance the outfit.

Check waist coverage while seated. Try the jacket with your winter sweater to assess room across the shoulders and arms.

## A longer coat for dinner and everyday wear

A longer coat can work with a midi dress or tailored trousers. Pair it with simple jewellery for the evening, or knitwear during the day. Do not judge length only while standing: walk, take ordinary steps and sit down in the coat.

## Fur collars and trims as smaller accents

If you like the texture without wanting an all-over fur surface, consider collar or trim details. A collar sits near the face, so look at the colour in natural light if possible. With cream outerwear, try brown, beige or charcoal accessories.

## A vest for layered outfits

The sleeves of the knit underneath a vest become part of the outfit. A solid-colour sweater keeps the combination straightforward. For extended outdoor exposure in cold weather, a coat that also covers the arms may be more appropriate.

## Understand the material, not just the appearance

Natural fur, shearling, faux fur and different trims can look similar in photographs. If composition, origin or animal welfare matters to you, ask for information about the individual garment and its care instructions. Do not use appearance or price as a material test.
