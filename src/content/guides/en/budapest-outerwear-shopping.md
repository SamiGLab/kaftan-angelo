> **Quick answer:** Kaftan Angelo is at Kossuth Lajos u. 18, Budapest 1053, between Astoria and Ferenciek tere. The Budapest store opened in 2004 and offers leather, shearling, fur and leather accessories.

## Before visiting
Browse the [collections](/en/collections/) and keep a screenshot of any model you want to see. The gallery is a selection, not a live stock system. If size, colour or a particular style matters, contact the store before travelling.

## What to bring
Bring the sweater or everyday layer you will wear underneath. Think about your normal use and prepare questions about outer material, lining, care, fit and availability. Photographs help identify the design, while a personal fitting checks comfort.

## During the fitting
Try the garment open and closed. Sit, reach forward and check sleeve coverage. Compare another cut where available. For custom leather-jacket enquiries, discuss style, colour, measurements and estimated timing individually; a website photo does not establish a production promise.

## Address and contact
- Address: **Kossuth Lajos u. 18, Budapest 1053**.
- Monday–Friday: **10:00–19:00**; Saturday: **10:00–17:00**; Sunday: closed.
- Telephone: **+36 1 266 7274**.

For a time-sensitive visit, confirm arrangements directly, especially around holidays. Use the [store page](/en/visit/) for the map and contact links.

## Common questions
**Can I reserve through the gallery?** Ask the store about availability and possible arrangements; the gallery itself is not a reservation.

**Does the store only sell leather jackets?** No. Shearling, fur and leather accessories are also part of the selection.
