> **Quick answer:** Choose a cut that works with your clothes and everyday movement. Check body length, shoulders, fastening and layering rather than relying on a body-shape category or size label.

## Start with how you will wear it
Are you choosing a jacket for walking, work or occasional wear? Bring the layer you normally wear underneath. Browse the [women’s leather collection](/en/womens-leather-jackets/) and keep a model photo; the gallery is not a live stock list.

## Shorter or longer?
Try a shorter jacket with your usual trousers or skirt. In a longer cut, check comfort while sitting and walking. Proportions are a personal preference; there is no compulsory rule for every body shape.

## Fitted or relaxed?
When fastened, the jacket should not pull uncomfortably at the chest, back or armholes. In a relaxed cut, still check sleeve coverage and shoulder comfort. If one area is tight and another loose, compare a different cut rather than only changing the size label.

## Details and your own style
Check the collar, belt and zip line open and closed. Bring your usual bag to check movement and where the shoulder strap sits. Ask about the individual model’s colour, material and finish.

## A fitting checklist
- Does it fasten over your normal layer?
- Is sitting, reaching and walking comfortable?
- Do the body and sleeve lengths suit you?
- Do you know the material, finish and care instructions?
- Is your preferred size and colour currently available?

## Common questions
**Should I size down because leather stretches?** Do not rely on stretching to fix uncomfortable fit.

**Can every cut be altered?** Discuss the individual garment and proposed adjustment with the store; the website does not promise alterations for every model.
