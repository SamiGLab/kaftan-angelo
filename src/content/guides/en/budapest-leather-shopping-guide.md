## 2. New Premium Boutique vs Vintage Thrift Shops in Budapest

While Budapest boasts numerous second-hand and vintage shops along Károly körút and the Jewish Quarter, shopping pre-owned garments carries distinct risks:

- **Leather Suppleness & Hygiene:** Vintage coats frequently suffer from dried-out grain, stale tobacco or musty storage smells, and worn-out linings. Necessary restorations (replacing linings, main zippers, and professional rehydration) often exceed the purchase price.
- **Guarantee of Freshness:** Kaftan Angelo exclusively provides brand-new, carefully selected premium leather garments. Every piece in our boutique is clean, structurally pristine, and immediately ready to wear.
- Review our dedicated [second-hand leather jacket checklist](/en/guides/second-hand-leather-jacket-checklist/) for practical risk assessment.
