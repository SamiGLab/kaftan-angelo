> **Before you visit:** Choose a style, send its product page and your preferred size to the store, and ask about availability. Visit Kaftan Angelo at Kossuth Lajos u. 18, 1053 Budapest to compare materials and try garments on.

A photograph helps you shortlist a jacket. A fitting lets you check its weight, shoulder fit and freedom of movement with the layers you plan to wear.

## Choose a collection

- [Women's leather jackets](/en/womens-leather-jackets/): compare colours, short and longer cuts, and reversible styles. See the [women's selection guide](/en/guides/choosing-womens-leather-jacket/).
- [Men's leather jackets](/en/mens-leather-jackets/): explore collared, quilted and biker-inspired styles. A fashion biker cut does not establish motorcycle protective certification.
- [Shearling jackets](/en/shearling-jackets/): check the individual garment's inner surface, coverage and room for winter layers.
- [Fur coats and vests](/en/fur-coats/): compare coats, shorter jackets and vests separately. Ask about the material and care of the specific piece.

Read [leather, shearling or fur](/en/guides/leather-shearling-fur-comparison/) for fitting considerations. Material names alone do not guarantee a particular warmth, weight or weather resistance.

## Prepare for a fitting

1. Keep the individual model's photo or page link.
2. Send it to [WhatsApp (+36 20 359 3216)](https://wa.me/36203593216) with your preferred size and colour. Ask about the current price and availability.
3. If you need a piece held for your visit, agree this explicitly with the store. An enquiry alone does not reserve it.
4. Bring the layer you intend to wear underneath. Fasten the jacket, sit down and reach forward to check movement.
5. Ask about the material label, care and any proposed sizing adjustment before deciding.

See [preparing for a store fitting](/en/guides/budapest-outerwear-shopping/) for more checks.

## Inspect the individual garment

When comparing new and second-hand clothing, inspect seams, fastenings, lining and surface, and ask about condition and care history. The category alone does not determine quality or repair costs. Our [second-hand checklist](/en/guides/second-hand-leather-jacket-checklist/) provides checks, not a condition assessment of any particular garment.

## Custom requests and common questions

For a particular size, colour or cut, discuss [custom order options](/en/custom-orders/) with the store. Agree materials, measurements, fitting steps and estimated timing for your request. Travellers should share their dates before agreeing arrangements.

**Do I need an appointment?** You can visit during opening hours. For a particular model or custom request, contact the store beforehand.

**Are all photographed models available?** The website presents a selection, not live inventory. The store must confirm size, colour and availability.

**Where can I ask about the price?** Product prices are not published here. Ask privately on WhatsApp or during your visit.
