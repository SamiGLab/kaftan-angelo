> **Quick answer:** Ask for material and condition details and close-up photos. Where possible, check stitching, lining, fastening and fit in person. Do not burn, soak or scratch the garment to identify its material.

This is a general buying checklist, not a description of a Kaftan Angelo second-hand collection or buying service.

## Material and history
Ask about the label, finish, previous cleaning, repairs and storage. A listing photo or the words “genuine leather” do not establish every material or quality claim. Read [recognising leather](/en/guides/how-to-tell-real-leather/).

## Surface and areas under strain
Inspect the collar, elbows, cuffs, pocket edges and fastenings in good light. Ask for close-ups of wear, cracking, discolouration or repaired areas. Do not automatically assume a mark or damage can be removed.

## Stitching, lining and fastenings
Check loose seams, tears, missing buttons and pulling in the lining. With permission, test the zip fully. Ask an appropriate specialist about the cost and feasibility of any repair before deciding; do not assume a guaranteed inexpensive restoration.

## Odour and cleaning questions
Odour may raise storage or cleaning questions, but does not prove leather authenticity. Ask about the history if a smell is strong or persistent. Do not soak the garment or use unknown solvents, oils or fragrances. Show the cleaner the care label.

## Fit and total cost
Ask for dimensions and measurement points as well as the size label. If a fitting is possible, try your usual layer while sitting and moving. Consider necessary cleaning and agreed repair costs alongside the purchase price; do not expect stretching to fix poor fit.

## Before deciding
Clarify condition and the particular purchase arrangements with the seller. Ask about flaws not visible in the photos. To compare new coats in person, browse [Kaftan Angelo collections](/en/collections/) and the [store page](/en/visit/); confirm current availability separately.
