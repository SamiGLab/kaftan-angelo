> **Quick answer:** Avoid direct heat, compression and spraying products onto fur. Ask a fur-care specialist about cleaning and seasonal storage rather than treating the garment like ordinary washable clothing.

## Identify what needs attention
Describe whether the problem concerns a spill, flattened hair, a seam, lining or fastening. Photograph the whole garment and the affected area in ordinary light. Include the material label and any leather panels when requesting advice.

## Give the garment space
The American Fur Council recommends appropriate specialist storage and warns against perfume and hairspray on fur. In your wardrobe, avoid continual compression and choose support suitable for the garment’s weight. Check the instructions before selecting a cover.

## Plan seasonal care
Inspect pockets, closures, lining and the places where a bag strap rubs. Ask about cleaning needs and available storage arrangements. A home wardrobe should not be assumed to reproduce a specialist storage environment. Do not improvise a freezer treatment.

## Prepare a useful enquiry
Tell the specialist what caused a mark and which products were already applied. Ask whether they handle that construction, what the process involves and whether existing damage affects the result. Discuss timing and costs for the actual garment.

## Common questions
**Can I spray perfume onto the coat?** Keep sprays away from the fur surface.

**Does every stain need the same cleaning?** No. The material and cause matter; seek advice before rubbing or applying a cleaner.

**Can a photo settle the treatment?** It can begin the enquiry, but a final assessment may require inspection. Ask the store about available assistance rather than assuming a particular service.
