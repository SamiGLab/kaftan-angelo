## Four checks to make in the fitting room

1. **Shoulders:** The shoulder line should sit naturally without pulling across the upper arm.
2. **Movement:** Fasten the jacket, reach forward and sit down. Check how it feels in each position.
3. **Layers:** Bring the shirt or jumper you expect to wear underneath.
4. **Length:** Look at sleeve and body length while standing and sitting.

If a garment might need an adjustment, discuss what is possible for **that specific piece**, including any charge and timing. Not every alteration suits every model.
