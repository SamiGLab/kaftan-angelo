When you compare two leather jackets, the lower price alone does not tell you which will be the better purchase. Think about which you would wear more often and what you know about its material, fit and care.

## What to compare

| Consideration | What to ask or check |
| --- | --- |
| Material | What does the label say about the outer material and lining? |
| Everyday use | Which works with the clothes you wear for work, weekends or travel? |
| Comfort | Can you move naturally, sit and reach forward? |
| Details | How do the zip, pockets and fastenings work? |
| Care | What cleaning and storage does this particular finish require? |

Neither a material name nor the price proves durability. Our [lambskin and cowhide comparison](/en/guides/lambskin-vs-cowhide-leather-jacket/) offers further questions.
