# Kaftan Angelo Project Rules & Brand Constraints

## Critical Business Constraints (Strictly Enforced)

1. **NO HANDCRAFTING ("El işçiliği YAPILMIYOR"):**
   - Kaftan Angelo is a premium boutique retail showroom in Budapest selling luxury genuine leather jackets, shearling coats, and fur styles.
   - **Strictly FORBIDDEN words:** "el işçiliği", "kézműves", "handcrafted", "artisan", "atelier", "hand-made".
   - The boutique provides personal fitting and tailored sizing adjustments ("egyedi rendelés", "méretre igazítás"), but never describe products as artisan handcrafting.

2. **NO TAX FREE ("Tax Free KESİNLİKLE YOK"):**
   - Kaftan Angelo does **NOT** provide Tax Free / VAT refund services.
   - **Strictly FORBIDDEN:** Do NOT suggest, advertise, badge, or add Tax Free, Global Blue, or VAT refund anywhere on the website, guides, or promotional materials.

3. **Core Business Model & KaftanDesk Safety:**
   - Goal: Drive store visits to Kossuth Lajos u. 18, Budapest and direct WhatsApp consultations.
   - No cart/checkout: Jackets and coats are high-ticket pieces that customers try on in store.
   - Zero modifications to Supabase database schema, RPC functions, or partner intake boundary without explicit authorization.

4. **No published product prices:**
   - Do not publish numeric product prices, starting prices or product price ranges in pages or structured data.
   - Customers may ask the store privately about current prices through WhatsApp or an in-store visit.
   - Keep factual Product/ItemPage information without Offer markup. Never invent prices, stock status or product ratings for search enhancements.
